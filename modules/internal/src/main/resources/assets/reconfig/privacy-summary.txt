Updated 20 October 2026

Local module settings, HUD positions, keybinds, themes, waypoints, policy acceptance and caches are stored on your computer. The Music HUD reads supported Spotify media-session metadata locally; it does not upload listening activity or inspect arbitrary browser titles. Reliable YouTube identification is not provided in this release.

After policy acceptance, social enrollment, presence and notification checks can run during gameplay even with Friends closed. The Cloudflare Worker/D1 service processes Minecraft username/UUID, hashes of random ReConfig authentication tokens, friend requests, friendships, messages, replies, timestamps, deletion records, presence and hashed server information. Minecraft ownership proof sends identity and a random serverId to the Worker, never your Minecraft access token or Microsoft password.

Profiles are private by default. Publishing your bio, tag and bundled emoji requires separate explicit consent. General policy acceptance does not publish your profile. A server invitation shares the full address with the invited friend. The public directory contains operator-approved listings, not private presence or invitation addresses.

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not send secrets. Removing a friend does not delete messages. Making a profile private hides publication but does not erase saved account data. There is no automatic retention-expiry/account-deletion scheduler. Message deletion removes its active body; synchronization tombstones and recipients' copies may remain.

Providers include Cloudflare Workers/D1, Netlify, Microsoft/Mojang, PlayerDB, Ashcon, MCHeads and GitHub. Profile/skin requests and hosting can expose identity/network metadata to those providers, including processing outside your country. Support reports and logs contain what you share. This notice does not guarantee security or a particular international-transfer arrangement.

ReConfig does not sell personal data or use behavioural advertising. Optional profile publication relies on consent; requested service operation, legitimate operational/security interests and applicable legal obligations support other purposes where relevant law applies.

Email privacy requests to therealduv14@gmail.com with your Minecraft name/UUID, never tokens or passwords. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, object, withdraw consent and complain to a data-protection authority. Identity verification may be needed. Requests require operator handling.

Young players should ask a parent/guardian before sharing personal information. No age-verification or parental-consent workflow is implemented. Follow applicable parental-authorization requirements. Closing Minecraft stops future requests; uninstalling may leave local settings and existing server data behind. Policy version changes require renewed acceptance.
